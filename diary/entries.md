## August 28, 2026 - 6:20am

We talked about how to label these notes.

The clearest term is "entry" or "session entry." It is simple, accurate, and flexible.

It fits a back-and-forth conversation without sounding too formal or too vague.

"Interaction" feels a little broad. "Conversation" is fine, but "entry" is better for a running log.

I added this note at the top so the newest record stays first.

## August 28, 2026 - 6:00am

The hard part was the challenge of randomizing a sentence while keeping it recognizable, readable, and faithful to the original wording.

I was trying to shuffle the words in a sentence without breaking it into nonsense, adding extra punctuation, changing the meaning, or making it look like a typo instead of a deliberate Dadaist variation.

The fix was to treat the sentence as a fixed set of words, reorder that set, and keep the punctuation pattern stable. That way the output still felt intentional and artistic, but it clearly had been randomized.

In plain terms: the sentence had to be scrambled enough to feel playful, but controlled enough to still be recognizably the same sentence.
